# Valuation model v3.1 — model card (delta to v3)

**Status: adopted by the owner on 2026-10-10 (shipped model; `src/lib/vardering/data/*` is generated from this directory).
A residual-based alternative (v3.2) was evaluated with the same protocol and rejected (branch `valuation-v3.2`,
`ml/artifacts/valuation-v3.2/evaluation.json`).** v3 (`ml/artifacts/valuation-v3/`) is untouched and remains the reference. Everything here is generated by
`ml/train_valuation_v31.py` (helpers `ml/valuation_v31_lib.py`, tests `ml/tests/test_valuation_v31.py`; seeded,
`RANDOM_STATE=42`, LightGBM `n_jobs=1`). Full numbers (all 40 candidates, every view): `metadata.json`.

Data, features, LightGBM, tuning protocol, smearing, conformal method and the effects (OLS) are **v3's, unchanged**:
`data/raw/Transaction data for valuation.xlsx`, contract dates 2026-01-01 to 2026-08-31, **n = 4,991** rows,
time split Jan–Jun (train, n=4,103) / Jul–Aug (test, **n=888**), `utgangspris` not a feature, no time feature.
The general (non-Stockholm) model, `general_model*.json` and `general_parity_fixtures.json` are byte-identical copies
of v3 (they use no postnummer); the Stockholm card sections 1, 3–6 of `valuation-v3/MODEL_CARD.md` still describe them.

## What changed and why

Trigger: owner report. Postnummer 11122 has exactly **one** training sale (2026-08-11, 48 m², 85,417 kr/m²). With v3's
`k = 0.5` that one sale gave `postcode_delta = −0.288` (≈ −25 % on every apartment in 11122, 78,015 kr/m² for the owner's
44 m² example vs 110–114 k in the neighbouring postnummer). The owner pointed out that 11122 really is cheaper (few homes,
office-dominated), so this is an **evidence question, not a confirmed bug**. The measurements below answer it.

**v3.1 changes only the `postcode_delta` encoding**:

```
v_i            = log(price_per_sqm_i) − mean log(price_per_sqm) of the row's omrade                (as v3)
prefix_table[q]= Σ_{i: postnummer starts with q} v_i / (n_q + 5)                      q = first 4 digits, k_group = 5
table[p]       = g + Σ_{i in p} (v_i − g) / (n_p + 5)                                 g = prefix_table[p[:4]], k = 5
unseen p       = prefix_table[p[:4]] if present, else 0.0                              (v3: always 0.0)
```

I.e. a postnummer is shrunk toward the mean of its **4-digit neighbourhood** (≈ the same street area), not toward the
whole omrade, and a postnummer with no sale of its own inherits its neighbourhood's delta instead of "average". Booster
inputs, inference formulas 1, 3–7 of the v3 card, smearing and interval formulas are unchanged. Only formula 2 changes
(table lookup → table, then prefix_table, then 0). `postcode_encoding.json` keeps `table` (keys = exactly the 650 training
postnummer; the build script relies on this) and adds `prefix_length` (4), `prefix_table` (106 four-digit prefixes with training sales) and
`k_group_shrinkage`. Hyper-parameters: the 40-config random search (same space/seed/selection as v3) picked the same
configuration as v3 (`n_estimators=250, num_leaves=31, lr 0.05, min_child_samples=10, reg_lambda=0, colsample 0.8,
subsample 0.85, max_depth 8`); adopted because Jul–Aug improved (9.83 vs default-params 10.02 %).

**Decision for the owner (touches "owner decision 5", unseen postnummer → delta 0):** the prefix fallback is what
removes the unseen-postnummer penalty (section 2). Shrinking seen postnummer harder *without* the fallback gains
nothing measurable (CV −0.07 pp, grouped CV −0.10 pp, both CIs include 0). The fallback is a data-driven change of the
unseen rule; the confidence tier is untouched ("no special confidence treatment" still holds).

**Reading it**

- **v3's small k is not "measurably worse" on the sparse strata with an omrade prior — larger k is worse there.** Simulated 1-sale MAPE
  (n=3,394): k=0.5 10.07, k=2 10.27, k=5 10.59, k=10 10.74, k=20 10.89 — i.e. when a postnummer has one sale, trusting that sale
  (relative to the omrade mean) predicts its *other* sales better than shrinking it hard toward the omrade. This supports the owner's
  point: a single sale in a postnummer carries real local signal. Dropping the delta when n is small (n_min) is worse (simulated MAPE 11.8–12.3, ≈ no delta).
- **The prior is the problem, not k.** The omrade is a coarse reference. Shrinking toward the 4-digit neighbourhood instead keeps
  (and improves) sparse-postnummer accuracy at *larger* k: simulated 1-sale 9.68 vs 10.07, 2-sale 9.37 vs 9.47.
- **The clear gain is for postnummer without any sale.** Grouped CV (whole postnummer held out; n=4,991): v3 **14.63 %**, no postcode feature 13.61 %,
  v3.1 11.87 % (Jan–Jun grouped: 14.52 / 13.77 / 11.93). **v3 is worse than ignoring the postnummer when the postnummer is unseen** (consistent
  with the v3 card's n=31 observation: 17.86 vs 15.07 %). The hierarchy *without* the prefix fallback gains nothing here (14.46 %).
- Overall accuracy does not suffer: v3.1 is 0.09 pp better in full CV and 0.16 pp better on Jul–Aug (default params).

### Selection rule (fixed before the final numbers; Jan–Jun data only)

1. keep candidates whose Jan–Jun 5-fold CV MAPE is within 0.10 pp of the best (10.066 %); 2. among them, the lowest
Jan–Jun grouped CV MAPE → **k=5, 4-digit prefix, k_group=5, unseen→prefix** (Jan–Jun CV 10.08, grouped 11.93). Neighbouring
settings (k 2–5, k_group 5–20) are within ≈ 0.1–0.3 pp of each other on every view: the surface is flat, so the exact choice is not
meaningful; Jul–Aug, the full CV and the simulation were not used to choose.

## 5. Effects / drivers

`effects.json` is the OLS companion and does not depend on the postnummer encoding: identical to v3 apart from `version` and `generated_at`
(checked in `test_valuation_v31.py` and `metadata.effects_vs_v3`). Same signs as v3 (`fee_per_sqm+10` −2.42 %, `floor+1` +1.64 %, `build_year+10` −1.52 %, `elevator=nej` −2.41 %, …); no
new sign flips. These are descriptive associations, not causal claims (CLAUDE.md rule); `drivare.ts` logic and its tests are unchanged and pass.

## Files, sizes, parity

- `model.json` 2,746,280 B (250 trees; v3 2,742,957 B), `postcode_encoding.json` 15,322 B (v3 13,063 B: table 650 entries + `prefix_table`), `general_model.json` 779,086 B; model+general+postcode **3,540,688 B** (budget 4.5 MB).
- `parity_fixtures.json`: 53 rows (50 data rows + 3 synthetic: unseen postnummer with a known 4-digit prefix → prefix delta; `99999` → 0; missing postnummer → 0). `postcode_delta` in fixtures uses the **rounded (6 d.p.) shipped tables**.
  Reference recipe (`ml/tests/test_valuation_v31.py`) reproduces all rows to 1e-9; the TS evaluator reproduces them (`parity.test.ts`). Native vs exported booster: max |Δ log price| 4.8e-05 (first 200 rows).
- Smearing factor 1.004271 (v3: 1.004473).
- TypeScript: `postcodeDeltaFor` = `table[p]` → `prefixTable[p.slice(0, 4)]` → 0.

## Limitations (new or changed vs v3)

- The prefix prior assumes neighbouring postnummer (same first 4 digits) price alike. Prefix groups straddle real borders (a 4-digit group is several streets; across a boundary it can mislead). Not tested at finer resolution.
- Grouped CV removes whole postnummer but a held-out postnummer's neighbours stay in training — the same situation as a buyer in a never-seen postnummer next to sold ones; it does **not** cover a buyer in a prefix with no sales (then delta = 0 as in v3; `n_prefix` in the file shows how many prefixes have evidence; for such postnummer expect the v3-like unseen error).
- 11122-type local effects are partially damped (see section 4). A real, strong micro-location effect backed by one sale is under-weighted by construction; it is weighted *more* than a large-k omrade encoding would.
- Coverage of the 90 % interval is 68–83 % for postnummer with 0–2 sales (unchanged problem from v3; not fixed).
- Sample is Jan–Aug 2026 only, as v3; repeat sales in the same building/BRF inflate CV for seen postnummer (the grouped CV and the simulation are the honest views for sparse/unseen postnummer).
